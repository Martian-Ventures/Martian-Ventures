Our Services

What we build

End-to-end products and platforms for energy and industrial operations.

Enforced Learning Management System (LMS)

A platform designed to train oil and gas industry workers on company policies, safety procedures and job responsibilities.

  • Mandatory Training Programs - ensuring all employees complete essential training before starting work.
  • Compliance & Certification Tracking - monitoring progress and ensuring regulatory compliance.
  • Performance Assessments & Reports - evaluating understanding and generating progress reports.
  • Interactive Learning Modules - videos, simulations and quizzes that enhance learning.

SCADA Systems

Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition systems provide real-time plant intelligence and centralized control for industrial operations - live monitoring, alarm management, operator HMIs and a data historian for full operational visibility.

Key capabilities include Performance Ratio & Yield analytics, historical trending, predictive maintenance and consolidated dashboards, with secure connectivity to edge devices over standard industrial protocols (Modbus, OPC UA, DNP3).

  • Real-time Plant Intelligence - live telemetry, dashboards and KPI monitoring.
  • Performance Ratio & Yield Analytics - track efficiency, losses and production yield.
  • Integration with EMS, BMS & Weather Stations - consolidated operational context.
  • Alarm Management & Historical Trending - prioritize events and analyze performance.
  • Predictive Maintenance & Data Historian - forecast failures with ML-ready datasets.
  • Secure, Scalable Connectivity - edge devices, role-based access and protocol support.
  • Customizable HMI & Reports - tailored views for operators, managers and analysts.

Predictive Maintenance Solutions

AI-driven solutions that analyze equipment performance to predict failures before they occur.

  • Sensor-Based Monitoring - IoT devices that track machine health in real time.
  • AI & Machine Learning Analytics - predicting failures and suggesting maintenance schedules.
  • Automated Alerts & Notifications - informing engineers when maintenance is needed.

Custom Software Development

Tailored software solutions to meet specific business needs across the energy sector and beyond.

  • Custom Web & Mobile Applications - built around your workflows.
  • System Integration - connecting the tools your teams already rely on.
  • Data Analytics & Business Intelligence - turning raw data into decisions.

Drone Surveillance Systems

Aerial monitoring and inspection for large sites and remote infrastructure. High-resolution and thermal cameras, RTK GPS and automated flight planning deliver fast situational awareness while reducing inspection time and operational risk.

Use cases include perimeter security, pipeline and asset inspections, emergency response support, progress monitoring and environmental surveys - integrated into SCADA, asset platforms and command dashboards.

  • High-resolution & Thermal Imaging - detailed visual and temperature inspections.
  • Automated Flight Plans & BVLOS Support - scheduled missions where permitted.
  • Live Video Streaming & Telemetry - real-time situational awareness for operators.
  • GIS Mapping & Change Detection - track site changes and generate orthomosaics.
  • Integration with SCADA / Asset Systems - aerial data into existing platforms.
  • Regulatory Compliance & Data Security - flight logging, encrypted storage and transfer.
